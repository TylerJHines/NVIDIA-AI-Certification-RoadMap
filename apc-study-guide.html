AI Power and Cooling — study guide outline

Based on your uploaded October 2026 candidate-facing draft. The draft explicitly states exam details are provisional and official publication should be checked.

AI Factory Fundamentals

  • AI factory purpose: Computing infrastructure that turns data into AI outputs
  • AI factory dependency: Power, cooling, controls, networking and compute work together
  • Credential boundary: Awareness does not grant switching or design authority
  • Role ownership: Use the current site responsibility matrix

Power Infrastructure Awareness

  • UPS purpose: Provides short-duration ride-through for designated loads
  • One-line diagram: Shows simplified sources, protection, distribution and loads
  • Redundant feeds: Two cords alone do not prove end-to-end independence
  • Abnormal electrical alarm: Record asset, status and time; escalate to authorized staff
  • Capacity terminology: Observed load and provisioned capacity are different

Cooling and Mechanical Awareness

  • CDU function: Transfers heat between technical and facility cooling loops
  • Direct liquid heat path: Cold plate to manifold to technical loop to CDU to facility loop
  • Condensation risk: Surfaces below dew point may condense moisture
  • Leak detection: Preserve evidence and escalate under site procedure
  • Cooling readings: Compare matching units, locations and operating states

Installation and Commissioning

  • Prefunctional check: Confirms prerequisites before functional testing
  • Integrated test: Verifies interactions during normal or failure scenarios
  • Missing approval: Hold the affected step and notify the responsible owner
  • Handover evidence: Approved checklists, test results, labels and deficiency closure
  • Cross-trade coordination: Ensure routes, clearances and access remain compatible

Operations and Maintenance

  • Alarm interpretation: An alarm flags a condition but does not prove root cause
  • MOP purpose: Controlled plan for a specific change or maintenance task
  • Trend evidence: Time series can help compare baselines and events
  • Preventive maintenance: Scheduled checks support reliability
  • Incident handoff: State asset, time, observation, actions, owner and next checkpoint

Safety and Communication

  • Hazardous energy: Authorized workers follow isolation and verification requirements
  • Unclear field condition: Stop affected work and escalate
  • Confidentiality: Use approved channels and protect customer/system details
  • Document revision: Confirm current approved revision before action
  • Communication: Separate observed facts from inferred causes
